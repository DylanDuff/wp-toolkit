# Viewing Form Entries

When someone submits a form on your site, their details are saved in WordPress so you can refer back to them at any time — even if the email notification got missed or went to spam.

---

## Finding Your Entries

1. Go to [**Fluent Forms**](admin.php?page=fluent_forms) in the left-hand admin menu.
2. You'll see a list of all forms on the site.
3. Click **Entries** beneath the form you want to check.

This opens a table of every submission that form has received.

You can also view submissions across all forms at once by clicking [**Fluent Forms → Entries**](admin.php?page=fluent_forms&route=entries) and using the dropdown to filter by form.

---

## Reading an Entry

Click the eye icon on any row to open the full submission and see every field the person filled in — name, email, message, and anything else the form asked for.

Use the **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the top to move between entries without going back to the list.

---

## Filtering & Searching

At the top of the entries list you can:

- **Search** by name, email, or any text from the submission
- **Filter by date** using preset ranges (Today, Last Week, Last Month) or a custom date range
- **Filter by status** — Unread, Read, Favourite, or Trashed

Entries are marked **Unread** until you open them, so the Unread filter is useful for catching anything you haven't reviewed yet.

---

## Marking Favourites

Click the star icon on any entry to mark it as a Favourite — useful for flagging something that needs follow-up. Use the **Favourite** filter to pull them back up later.

---

## Adding Notes

You can add internal notes to any entry — handy for recording follow-up actions or leaving context for someone else on the team.

Open an entry and look for the **Notes** section on the right-hand side. Notes are only visible in the admin and are never shown to the person who submitted the form.

---

## Bulk Actions

To act on multiple entries at once, tick the checkboxes on the left of each row, then use the **Bulk Actions** dropdown to:

- Delete selected entries
- Mark as read or unread
- Mark as favourite or remove from favourites
- Export selected entries

---

## Exporting Entries

If you need to share entries or work with them in a spreadsheet:

1. From the entries list, click **Export Entries** (top right).
2. Choose a format — **CSV** or **XLSX** for spreadsheets, **ODS** for LibreOffice, or **JSON** for developers.
3. The file will download to your computer.

Apply filters before exporting if you only want a specific date range or status.

---

## Deleting an Entry

To remove a single entry, open it and click **Delete** — or from the list view, hover over the row and click **Delete**.

Deleted entries cannot be recovered, so only remove something if you're sure you no longer need it.

> Deleting an entry from WordPress does not unsend any email notifications that were already delivered.
