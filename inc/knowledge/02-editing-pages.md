# Editing Pages

Pages on this site are built with **Bricks Builder**, a visual editor that lets you edit content directly on the page — what you see is what you get.

Your account has **editor-level access** to Bricks, which means you can update text, images, and content freely. Layout, structure, and styling controls are not available at this access level — this is intentional and keeps the design consistent while you work.

---

## Opening a Page to Edit

1. Go to [**Pages**](edit.php?post_type=page) in the left-hand admin menu.
2. Find the page you want to edit.
3. Hover over the page title and click **Edit with Bricks**.

This opens the visual editor in a new tab.

> If you click **Edit** instead of **Edit with Bricks**, you'll land in the standard WordPress editor. Close that tab and use **Edit with Bricks** from the Pages list instead.

---

## Editing Text

1. Click on any text element — it will get a blue outline to show it's selected.
2. **Double-click** to enter typing mode and make your changes.
3. Click elsewhere on the page when you're done.

---

## Swapping an Image

1. Click the image you want to change.
2. In the panel on the left, click the current image thumbnail.
3. The media library opens — select an existing image or upload a new one.
4. Click **Select** to apply it.

---

## Saving

Click **Save** in the top-right corner, or press `Ctrl + S` (Windows) / `Cmd + S` (Mac).

Changes go live immediately on any page that is already published — there is no separate publish step once a page is live.

---

## Revisions

Bricks keeps a history of saved versions for each page. If you need to undo a change you've already saved:

1. Click the **Revisions** icon in the top toolbar (clock icon).
2. Browse previous versions and click one to preview it.
3. Click **Apply** to restore that version.

---

## What You Can and Can't Edit

| | Available to you |
|---|---|
| Editing text and copy | Yes |
| Swapping images | Yes |
| Viewing and restoring revisions | Yes |
| Moving or adding sections | No — layout is locked |
| Changing colours, fonts, or spacing | No — styling is locked |
| Global Settings or Theme Styles | No — admin only |

Layout and styling controls are hidden at your access level. If something on the page needs a structural or design change, contact your developer.
