# Scheduling & Drafts

Not everything needs to go live the moment you write it. WordPress lets you save work as a draft to come back to later, schedule content to publish automatically at a set time, and take live content offline temporarily without deleting it.

---

## Saving a Draft

When creating or editing any page or collection entry, you can save your work without making it public.

- Click **Save Draft** instead of **Publish** to save without publishing.
- Drafts are only visible in the admin — they won't appear on the site until published.
- You can come back to a draft at any time by finding it in the relevant list and clicking to edit it.

To find your drafts, go to the relevant section (e.g. **Blog** or **Services**) and use the **Drafts** filter link at the top of the list.

---

## Scheduling Content to Publish Later

Instead of publishing immediately, you can set a future date and time for content to go live automatically.

1. Open the page or entry you want to schedule.
2. In the top-right panel, find the **Publish** section and click **Immediately** next to the publish date.
3. Set the date and time you want it to go live.
4. Click **Schedule** — the button will change from **Publish** to **Schedule** once a future date is set.

The content will publish automatically at the time you set. You'll see it listed as **Scheduled** in the admin until then.

---

## Taking Live Content Offline Temporarily

If you need to hide a published page or entry without deleting it — for example, a seasonal offer or an event that has passed — you can revert it to a draft.

1. Open the published page or entry.
2. In the top-right panel, click the **Status** dropdown and change it from **Published** to **Draft**.
3. Click **Update**.

The content is immediately removed from the site but remains in your admin for whenever you need it again.

---

## Previewing Before Publishing

To see how a draft or scheduled entry will look on the site before it goes live:

1. Open the draft or entry.
2. Click **Preview** in the top-right corner.
3. A new tab will open showing the content as it will appear to visitors.

> Preview links are temporary and only visible to logged-in users. They can't be shared with someone who doesn't have a site login.
