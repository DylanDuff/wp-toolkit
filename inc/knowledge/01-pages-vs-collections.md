# Pages vs. Collections

There are two types of content on this site: **Pages** and **Collections**. They're edited in different places and work differently — knowing which is which will save you a lot of time.

---

## Pages

Pages are one-off pieces of content — things like your **Home**, **About**, or **Contact** page. Each page has its own unique layout and exists once on the site.

**Pages are edited visually using Bricks Builder.**

To edit a page:

1. Go to [**Pages**](edit.php?post_type=page) in the left-hand admin menu.
2. Hover over the page title and click **Edit with Bricks**.
3. Click directly on any text or image in the editor to select and edit it.
4. Click **Save** when you're done.

> See the **Editing Pages** article for a full walkthrough.

---

## Collections

Collections are groups of similar content — things like **Blog Posts**, **Services**, **Team Members**, or **Case Studies**. Each item in a collection follows the same structure and often powers multiple pages on the site using a shared layout.

**Collections are edited as individual entries through structured forms in the WordPress admin (Gutenberg editor).**

Because one layout is reused across every item in the collection, editing is done through fields rather than a visual editor — this keeps things consistent and prevents one entry from looking different to all the others.

To edit a collection:

1. Find the collection in the left-hand admin menu (e.g. **Services**, **Blog**).
2. Click an existing entry to edit it, or click **Add New** to create one.
3. Update the fields — title, text, images, etc.
4. Click **Update** (or **Publish** for a new entry) when you're done.

> See the **Managing Content** article for a full walkthrough of working with fields and entries.

## Quick Reference

| | Pages | Collections |
|---|---|---|
| **Examples** | Home, About, Contact | Blog posts, Services entries, Team Members |
| **How many** | One of each | Many entries, one shared layout |
| **Edited with** | Bricks Builder (visual) | Gutenberg (structured fields) |
| **Found under** | [Pages](edit.php?post_type=page) | Their own menu item |
| **Layout changes** | Unique per page | Controlled by your developer |

---

## When in Doubt

If you're not sure whether something is a Page or a Collection, check the left-hand menu. Pages live under [**Pages**](edit.php?post_type=page); Collections have their own named menu entries. If you can't find what you're looking for, ask your developer.
