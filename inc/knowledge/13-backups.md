# Backups

Your site is backed up automatically — you don't need to do anything to keep this running. This article explains how it works and what to do if you ever need something restored.

---

## How Your Site is Backed Up

Your site is backed up from two independent locations. If one ever has an issue, the other acts as a fallback — meaning your data is always recoverable.

Backups run on the following schedule:

| Frequency | Retained for |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | 1 week |
| **Weekly** | 1 month |
| **Monthly** | 1 year |

This means if something goes wrong today, we can restore from yesterday. If something was missed for a few weeks, we can go back further. And for major issues, we have monthly snapshots going back a full year.

---

## What's Included in a Backup

Every backup captures the complete state of your site — all pages, content, media, settings, and the database. A restored backup brings everything back exactly as it was at that point in time.

---

## Requesting a Restore

If you need something restored — whether it's a page that was accidentally deleted, content that was overwritten, or a more serious issue — get in touch and let us know:

- **What** needs to be restored
- **When** it last looked correct (approximate date is fine)

We'll take it from there. In most cases a restore is straightforward and can be done quickly.

> The sooner you let us know about an issue, the more options we have. If you notice something is wrong, don't wait.

---

## A Note on Manual Changes

Backups protect against accidents and technical failures, but they restore everything to a previous state — including undoing any changes made after that point. If you've made updates you want to keep, let us know when requesting a restore and we'll work out the best approach.
