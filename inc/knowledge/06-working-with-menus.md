# Working with Menus

Menus control the navigation links on your site — things like the items in the header or footer. You can add, remove, and reorder links without touching any page layouts.

---

## Finding the Menu Editor

1. Go to [**Appearance → Menus**](nav-menus.php) in the left-hand admin menu.
2. Use the dropdown at the top to select the menu you want to edit, then click **Select**.

Your site may have more than one menu — for example, a main navigation menu and a separate footer menu. Make sure you've selected the right one before making changes.

---

## Adding a Page to the Menu

1. In the **Add menu items** panel on the left, select **Pages**.
2. Tick the pages you want to add.
3. Click **Add to Menu**.
4. The pages will appear at the bottom of the menu structure on the right.
5. Drag them into the position you want.
6. Click **Save Menu**.

You can also add custom links (for external URLs) or collection entries using the same panel on the left.

---

## Reordering Menu Items

Drag and drop items in the menu structure to change their order. To make an item a **sub-menu item** (a dropdown under another link), drag it slightly to the right underneath its parent.

---

## Removing a Menu Item

1. Click the arrow on the right of the menu item to expand it.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. Click **Save Menu**.

> Removing a menu item does not delete the page — it only removes the link from the navigation.

---

## Renaming a Menu Item

By default, menu items use the page title as their label. To display something different:

1. Click the arrow on the right of the item to expand it.
2. Update the **Navigation Label** field.
3. Click **Save Menu**.

This only changes how the link appears in the menu — it does not rename the page itself.
