# Welcome to Your Website

Your site has been built and configured — everything is set up and ready to go. This knowledge base is here if you ever want to make updates yourself, but there's no pressure to. If you'd rather just send us a message, we're always happy to take care of things for you.

---

## How Your Site is Organised

Your site has three types of content:

- **Pages** — one-off pages like your Home, About, and Contact page. Each has its own unique layout edited visually.
- **Collections** — groups of content like Services or Team Members that share a common structure, edited through fields in the admin.
- **Media** — the images, documents, and files that live in your Media Library and are used across the site.

Not sure about the difference between pages and collections? The [Pages vs Collections](?page=ddwpt-knowledge&doc=01-pages-vs-collections) article explains it clearly.

## Things Worth Knowing

- **You can't break the site by editing content.** Text, images, collection entries, and site settings are all safe to update. Layout and design are locked, so changes you make won't affect the look of the site.
- **Changes to pages go live immediately.** There's no staging step once a page is published — save when you're happy with the result.
- **Everything else is taken care of.** Backups run daily, updates are managed, and the site is monitored. You don't need to worry about the technical side.
